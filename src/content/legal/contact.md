## 2. Reporting Issues Efficiently

To help our technical team investigate and resolve issues swiftly, please include the following details in your email:

1. **URL**: The specific web address where the issue occurred (e.g., `https://komikhq.com/komik/solo-leveling/chapter-10`).
2. **Device & Browser**: Device model, operating system (e.g. Android, iOS, Windows, macOS), and browser type/version.
3. **Description**: A brief explanation of the problem (e.g., blank image, login error, layout overflow).
4. **Screenshots**: Optional, but highly appreciated for rapid debugging.

## 3. Cookie & Privacy Choices

If you wish to change your privacy settings or manage cookies for Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, you can do so directly on our website without sending an email by clicking the **"Cookie Settings"** button in the footer of any page.
