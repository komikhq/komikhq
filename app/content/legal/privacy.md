Welcome to **KomikHQ** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, your rights, and how third-party technologies (including Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and advertising networks) operate on our platform.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this policy, please contact us at **contact@komikhq.com**.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Credentials**: When you create an account, we collect your email address, display name, and securely hashed passwords (managed via Better Auth).
- **User Preferences**: Your reading history, bookmarks, favorite manga, and theme preferences (Dark/Light mode).
- **Communications**: If you contact us directly at **contact@komikhq.com**, we receive your email address, name, and the contents of your message.

### B. Information Collected Automatically

- **Device and Browser Data**: Browser type, operating system, device model, screen resolution, and language settings.
- **Log and Usage Data**: Pages viewed, manga chapters read, time spent on pages, referral sources, and IP address (anonymized/truncated for analytics).
- **Interaction Data**: Click maps, scroll depth, and interaction heatmaps via privacy-preserving session tools.

## 2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

KomikHQ uses cookies and similar storage technologies (such as `localStorage`) to maintain essential site functionality and analyze user engagement.

### Cookie Categories:

1. **Strictly Necessary (Essential) Cookies**:
   - Required for core platform functionality: user authentication, session security, CSRF token validation, and theme storage.
   - These cookies cannot be disabled as the site cannot function properly without them.

2. **Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics 4)**:
   - We use Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: `G-V44GMCRBP2`) to measure anonymous visitor traffic, popular manga trends, and system performance.
   - Google Analytics collects aggregated, non-personally identifiable statistics.
   - Google Analytics respects **Google Consent Mode v2**. If you decline analytics cookies, data storage is blocked.

3. **Experience & Heatmap Cookies (Microsoft Clarity)**:
   - We partner with Microsoft Clarity (Project ID: `yuh9101y1j`) to capture anonymous behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replay to improve website navigation and usability.
   - Sensitive text and private data are automatically masked.
   - Clarity honors user consent choices and will not store behavioral cookies if you decline.

4. **Third-Party Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense & Partners)**:
   - Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to KomikHQ or other websites.
   - Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
   - You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) or [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

## 3. How You Can Manage Your Cookie Preferences

You have full control over non-essential cookies on KomikHQ.

You can review or change your cookie preferences at any time by:

1. Clicking the **"Manage Preferences"** button on our Cookie Consent banner, or
2. Using the **"Cookie Settings"** link located in the footer of every page.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We process your data for the following purposes:

- To provide, operate, and maintain the KomikHQ reading platform.
- To preserve your bookmarks, reading progress, and account settings.
- To detect, prevent, and address technical glitches, security vulnerabilities, or abusive activities.
- To understand audience preferences and optimize comic viewing performance.
- To comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We **never sell** your personal information. We only share information with trusted service providers under strict data privacy terms:

- **Cloudflare**: Content delivery network, DDoS mitigation, and serverless hosting infrastructure.
- **Google & Microsoft**: Anonymized web analytics and session quality insights (subject to your consent).
- **Neon Postgres**: Encrypted database infrastructure for user accounts and bookmarks.

## 6. Data Retention and Security

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. We implement industry-standard security safeguards (HTTPS encryption, hashed passwords, CORS controls) to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

- **Right to Access**: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to Rectification**: Request correction of inaccurate information.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**: Request deletion of your account and associated reading data.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent**: Revoke consent for analytics or marketing cookies at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at **contact@komikhq.com**.
